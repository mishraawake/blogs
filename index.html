Weird Data Collections

A curated directory of unusual, top-level public datasets — the kind you'd never find via a generic search, but which power surprising work once you know they exist.

Museums & Cultural Heritage

  • Met Museum Open Accessgithub.com/metmuseumEvery artwork in the Metropolitan Museum's collection, full metadata, released as CC0.
  • Smithsonian Open Accesssi.edu4.5M items across 21 museums and the National Zoo, all CC0 — paintings, fossils, spacecraft components.
  • Rijksmuseum APIdata.rijksmuseum.nlHigh-resolution scans and metadata for Vermeer, Rembrandt, and 700,000 other Dutch artifacts.
  • MoMA Collectiongithub.com/MuseumofModernArtMoMA's entire artwork + artist database, published as JSON and CSV in a public Git repo.

Science & Earth

  • CERN Open Dataopendata.cern.chActual particle collision events from the Large Hadron Collider — the same data physicists publish papers from.
  • GBIFgbif.org2.6 billion species occurrence records — every documented sighting of every catalogued organism on Earth.
  • USGS Earthquake Feedearthquake.usgs.govEvery earthquake worldwide, streamed as JSON within minutes of detection.
  • NASA Open Data Portaldata.nasa.govTelemetry, planetary imagery, exoplanet catalogs, and several thousand more datasets from every NASA mission.

Web, Language & Knowledge

  • Common Crawlcommoncrawl.orgPetabytes of the open web, re-crawled monthly — the corpus most modern language models start from.
  • Wikidatawikidata.orgA structured knowledge graph linking every Wikipedia article — queryable via SPARQL, downloadable in full.
  • Internet Archivearchive.orgBooks, audio, video, software, and a near-complete archive of the public web going back to 1996.
  • Project Gutenberggutenberg.orgFull text of 70,000+ public-domain books, downloadable as plain text and EPUB.

Society, Maps & Records

  • GDELTgdeltproject.orgEvery news event happening anywhere on the planet, tagged by actor, location, and tone — updated every 15 minutes.
  • OpenCorporatesopencorporates.comThe largest open database of registered companies and their ownership, across 140+ jurisdictions.
  • Our World in Dataourworldindata.orgLong-run development data — life expectancy since 1543, energy use since 1800, every metric carefully cited.
  • OpenStreetMap Planetplanet.openstreetmap.orgThe full vector map of Earth as a 150 GB file — every road, building, and bench on the planet.

Curious & Specialized

  • Million Song Datasetmillionsongdataset.comAudio features and metadata for one million contemporary tracks — tempo, key, danceability, no audio itself.
  • The Pudding Datagithub.com/the-puddingSource datasets behind The Pudding's visual essays — hip-hop vocabularies, film dialogue gender splits, more.
  • Our Airportsourairports.comEvery airport, heliport, and seaplane base on Earth — 79,000 rows, CSV, kept current by volunteers.
  • David Rumsey Map Collectiondavidrumsey.com100,000+ historic maps from the 16th century onward, high-res scans with full metadata.

Inclusion criterion: the dataset must be (1) freely downloadable or query-able, (2) genuinely top-level, not a sliver of someone else's archive, and (3) hard to stumble onto from a generic search. Suggestions welcome.
